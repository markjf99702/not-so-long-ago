    About the bars

    Where the dates come from

    Every date here was checked against public sources: court rulings, statutes, federal agency notices, and the Wikipedia articles that cite them. State dates for sodomy laws mark when consenting adults of the same sex could no longer be prosecuted there, whether a legislature repealed the law or a court struck it down. State marriage dates mark when couples could marry statewide for good; short windows that were later shut are listed as their own events.

    Each lifeline takes a stripe of Gilbert Baker's 1978 rainbow flag, oldest person first. Baker said red stood for life.
